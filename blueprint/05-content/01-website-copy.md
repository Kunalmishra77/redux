### The problem, in hotel terms
> A guest reports a dripping tap. Engineering fixes it. Three weeks later the same room is on the
> list again. The model was discontinued in 2019, so the spare is either expensive or not quite
> right. Meanwhile the finish has scaled, and the brand standard audit is next month.
>
> Replacing every fitting is capex. Repeated makeshift repair is a recurring cost with no end.
> Restoration is the option between them.

### What a hotel engagement looks like
> **1 · Free assessment.** Our surveyor audits each fitting room by room — type, brand, model,
> finish and condition — photographed from four angles. You receive a written scope with three
> prices per fitting: restore, replace with Eurobrass, or replace at market.
>
> **2 · A pilot bathroom.** Before committing, take one bathroom. Agreed work, agreed cost, agreed
> timing. Judge the function, the finish and the downtime for yourself.
>
> **3 · The wider project.** Once the pilot is approved, we plan the rest in batches around dates
> your team provides.

### Downtime
> Removal, refitting, access and room availability are agreed before work begins. Your onboarded
> maintenance panel handles any required civil work — walls or tiles — agreed with REDUX in advance.
>
> Every room is checked for leaks, tested for operation and inspected for finish before it returns
> to service.

## Words we use, and words we don't

| Use | Don't use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Restoration | Refurbishment, reconditioning | Client's brand word |
| Free assessment | Site visit, inspection, survey *(externally)* | "Free" is the offer |
| Fitting | Fixture, tap-ware | Client's word |
| Suitable fittings | All fittings | Honest — and it is what the deck says |
| May have more service life | Will last years longer | Never over-promise on a condition-dependent outcome |
| Eurobrass can re-machine | We make any part | Accurate |
| Restore the finish | Re-chrome, polishing | Covers chrome and PVD |
